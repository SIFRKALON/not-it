# Not It

A Lacanian dating game set in New York City.

A bagel is born with a hole in the middle and spends the night trying to fill it. Pick dates on a risograph map of the city (a muscle-bound schmear on the Lower East Side, your own reflection in SoHo, a date with Times Square itself, fifty-seven swipes on the L train). Every one gets stamped **NOT IT**. Along the way a little twinkle keeps showing up just out of reach. You can chase it. You can't catch it.

Run out of fantasies, and the city opens up somewhere you weren't looking.

**Play:** open `index.html` in any browser. Everything (art, animation, music, sound) is generated in the page; there are no assets to load besides fonts.

- **✦ Play** is the game: the map, the dates, the stamps, the chase.
- **▶ Watch** is the original short film, [The Hole Story](https://sifrkalon.github.io/the-hole-story/), start to finish.

Each scene has a tap-to-open note on the bit of Lacan it's playing with: the Other, the mirror stage, anxiety as the lack of lack, desire as metonymy, the objet petit a, the gaze, tuché, and the Borromean knot.
